# kootu product context

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Passengers arriving at an airport need to enter their arrival, destination and explicit travel needs, then find a compatible shared ride. Drivers manage availability and approved vehicles. Operators approve drivers and vehicles. These roles and workflows are documented in README.md and implemented in the workspace components.

## Brand personality

Calm, practical and welcoming. Preserve the existing green and warm neutral identity. Use direct instructions about the next action, as in the booking form; avoid generic promotional or AI-generated filler.

## Anti-references

The user explicitly rejects unreadable transparent glass surfaces and confusing authentication steps. Do not imply that a booking, provider request or approval succeeded before it has.

## Design principles

- Make the next step obvious and return people to their chosen workspace.
- Keep authentication readable, including errors and secondary actions.
- Let users review extracted flight details and transcribed needs.
- Explain real waiting, configuration and approval states honestly.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Preserve the existing keyboard focus, skip links, labelled fields and reduced-motion support. Use opaque form surfaces and contrasting text. Support narrow screens without hiding essential actions.
